About the Island

Welcome to Taniti

Taniti is a small tropical island in the Pacific. Although the island has an area of less than 500 square miles, its geography is varied and includes sandy and rocky beaches, a small but safe harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior that includes a small, active volcano.

Taniti City features native architecture and nearby white sandy beaches surrounding beautiful Yellow Leaf Bay.

Getting Around Taniti

Once you arrive, Taniti provides several ways to travel between the city, lodging, attractions and entertainment areas.

🚌

Public Buses

Serve Taniti City from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. every day.

🚐

Private Buses

Private buses serve the rest of the island.

🚕

Taxis

Taxis are available throughout Taniti City.

🚗

Rental Cars

Cars can be rented from a local rental agency near the airport.

🚲

Bicycles

Bikes and helmets are available from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.

Walking: Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. Many tourists stay in the area surrounding Merriton Landing, which is also easy to explore on foot.
Know Before You Go

Frequently Requested Information

Important information visitors should know before and during their trip to Taniti.

🔞 What is the drinking age?

The drinking age on Taniti is 18 , and the drinking age is not strictly enforced.

⚡ What voltage does Taniti use?

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

🍺 When can alcohol be purchased?

Alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

🗣️ Is English spoken on Taniti?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

🏥 What medical services are available?

There is one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

🛡️ Is Taniti safe?

Violent crime is very rare on Taniti. However, as tourism increases, there have been more reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes.

📅 What should I know about holidays?

Taniti enjoys a large number of national holidays. Many tourist attractions and restaurants will be closed on holidays, so visitors should plan accordingly.

💵 What currency does Taniti use?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Several banks facilitate currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.
